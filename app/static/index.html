    About Water Storage Site Finder

    Developed by Junaid Ahmad, PhD. © 2026.

    Water Storage Site Finder screens locations for storage dams and water-harvesting structures anywhere in the world using open data only. For a point it traces the catchment, estimates rainfall, runoff, floods, silt and evaporation, tests a range of dam heights, checks land cover, settlements, protected areas and earthquakes, and gives a 0–100 feasibility score with a PDF report. It can also search a whole area and rank the best sites.

    Data sources

    • Terrain: Copernicus GLO-30 / GLO-90 DEM (ESA / Airbus)
    • Land cover: ESA WorldCover 2021; ESA CCI Land Cover 1992–2020; Impact Observatory / Esri 10 m annual maps
    • Surface water: JRC Global Surface Water (European Commission)
    • Rainfall & evaporation: ERA5 via Open-Meteo; NASA POWER as backup
    • Soil: ISRIC SoilGrids · Earthquakes: USGS · Places, dams, protected areas & place search: © OpenStreetMap contributors
